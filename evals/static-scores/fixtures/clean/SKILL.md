# CSV cleaner

## Trim whitespace
Strip leading and trailing whitespace from every cell.

## Remove duplicate rows
Keep the first occurrence of each row. Report how many were removed.

## Normalize dates
Convert date cells to ISO 8601. Leave unparseable cells unchanged and list them.
