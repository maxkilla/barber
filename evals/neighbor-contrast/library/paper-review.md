# Paper review (peer review of someone else's submission)
## Format
Summary, strengths, weaknesses, questions for authors, score with confidence.
## Checks
Verify claims against the experiments table. Flag missing baselines and unreported variance.
## Tone
Specific and civil. Never guess the authors' identity.
