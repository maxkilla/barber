# Academic paper writer (humanities essays)
## Argument
State a thesis in the first paragraph. Each section advances one claim with evidence.
## Citations
Chicago notes-bibliography style. Footnotes, not parenthetical cites. Primary sources before secondary.
## Revision
Read aloud once. Cut every sentence that doesn't serve the thesis.
## Tone
Avoid first person unless the discipline expects it.
